  ---
  The problem

  Phone calls shouldn't still be this hard.

  You need to book an appointment. Check on an order. Sort out a billing issue. But the number goes to a menu, the menu goes to hold
  music, and hold music goes nowhere for twenty minutes.

  Everything else in your life moved online. The phone call didn't.

  ---
  What Dialact does

  You tell it the task. It makes the call.

  Dialact dials the number, listens to the menu, presses the right options, and speaks naturally with whoever picks up — whether that's an
   automated system or a real person. When it's done, you get a summary of exactly what happened.

  No waiting. No hold music. No explaining yourself twice.

  ---
  How it works

  1. Describe the task
  "Call my electricity provider and ask why my bill jumped this month."

  2. Dialact makes the call
  It navigates the phone tree, waits on hold so you don't have to, and handles the conversation in real time.

  3. You get the outcome
  A clear summary of what was said, what was resolved, and what (if anything) needs to happen next.

  ---
  Built for real calls

  Most phone systems weren't designed to be friendly. Dialact was designed to handle them anyway.

  It understands complex IVR menus and navigates them automatically. It responds naturally with under half a second of delay — fast enough
   to hold a real conversation without awkward pauses. And if anything unexpected comes up, you can jump in and take over at any time.

  ---
  Use cases

  Personal
  Booking appointments. Disputing charges. Following up on deliveries. The calls you keep putting off because they take too long.

  For non-native speakers
  Call in any language. Dialact can speak to the other end in their language and report back to you in yours.

  For businesses
  Automate high-volume outbound calls — reminders, confirmations, surveys — without a call centre.

  ---
  You stay in control

  Dialact isn't a black box. You can watch the call as it happens and read a live transcript in real time. If you want to step in, you
  can. If you're happy to let it run, you don't have to do a thing.
