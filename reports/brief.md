  Dialact — Landing Page Brief

  Product

  Dialact is an AI agent that makes phone calls for you. Give it a task — book an appointment,
  navigate a phone menu, follow up on an order — and it handles the call start to finish, in
  real time.

  Problem It Solves

  Phone calls are the last frontier of digital friction. Everything else can be done online,
  but millions of tasks still require you to sit on hold, press 1 for English, listen to a
  menu, and talk to a representative. It's slow, annoying, and hard to delegate.

  Dialact removes that friction entirely.

  How It Works (User-Facing)

  1. You describe the task — "Call my electricity provider and ask about my account balance" or
   "Book a haircut for Saturday afternoon."
  2. Dialact makes the call — it dials the number, navigates phone menus automatically, and
  converses naturally with whoever picks up.
  3. You get the outcome — a summary of what happened, what was said, what was resolved.

  You never have to wait on hold again.

  Key Value Props

  - Hands-free — no hovering required, it runs on its own
  - Instant — sub-400ms response latency, sounds natural
  - Smart navigation — understands and navigates IVR phone trees automatically
  - Multilingual — can call in one language and report back in another
  - Supervised — you can monitor live and take over at any time

  Target Audience

  - Busy professionals who want to outsource annoying calls
  - People with phone anxiety
  - Non-native speakers navigating foreign-language phone systems
  - Businesses that need to automate high-volume outbound calls (follow-ups, reminders,
  surveys)

  Tone

  Calm. Capable. Trustworthy.
  Not gimmicky. Not overhyped. The product is genuinely impressive — let the concept speak.
  Think: "This just works."

  Suggested Headline Directions

  - "We'll make the call."
  - "Your AI that handles the phone call."
  - "Stop waiting on hold. Let AI handle it."
  - "Phone calls, handled."

  Key Sections for the Landing Page

  1. Hero — headline + sub-headline + CTA (waitlist / try it)
  2. How it works — 3-step visual (describe → call happens → get outcome)
  3. Demo or animation — show a live call in progress (waveform, transcript)
  4. Use cases — booking appointments, navigating customer service, business outbound
  5. Trust signals — latency stats, multilingual, human takeover available
  6. CTA — waitlist or early access
